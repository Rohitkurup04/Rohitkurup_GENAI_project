Questions

Are the prices exact?

No. Costs are rough per-person estimates in rupees. Check current prices before you book.

Is this using a live AI model?

Not yet. This version matches your words against a built-in list of places. A live model can be connected later through a backend.

Can I plan a destination that isn’t listed?

Yes, you’ll get general stops. Detailed guides exist for the five destinations shown.
